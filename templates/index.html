THE EIGHT RANKS

Everyone starts at 1000. Beating a stronger opponent is worth more than beating a weaker one, and killing the same player over and over is worth less each time. Ratings stay provisional (~) until three recorded fights.

MORE FROM THEREALNOOB

ELO System RELEASED

Kill-based Elo for any PvP server. Ranks, killstreaks, anti-farming, tab-list tags — entirely client-side.

DOWNLOAD

Linked IN DEVELOPMENT

One health bar for everyone: damage hits all of you, and when it runs out you all die together. Plus a shared bag — one player puts something in, anyone can take it out. Built for co-op runs.

Orphaned IN DEVELOPMENT

Removed a mod from your world? Minecraft silently deletes every block it owned. Orphaned finds those leftovers and cleans them up.

Map Marker IN DEVELOPMENT

Mark out a region with two key presses, give it a name and a colour, and group regions by that colour.

ChunkView IN DEVELOPMENT

Render distance far beyond the vanilla limit, set with a command: /rd <chunks>.

AI Builder IN DEVELOPMENT

Describe a building in plain text and watch it get placed block by block.

SUPPORT THE PROJECT

Every mod here is free, has no ads and collects nothing you did not agree to. Running the leaderboard costs nothing today — it sits on free hosting — and the plan is to keep it that way.

If you want to help anyway, the things that actually move the needle are free:

★ Leave a rating

⚑ Tell your server
