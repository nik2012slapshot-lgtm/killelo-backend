Support the project

Every mod here is free, has no ads and collects nothing you did not agree to. Running the leaderboard costs nothing today, it sits on free hosting, and the plan is to keep it that way.

If you want to help anyway, the things that actually move the needle are free:

Leave a rating

A follow or a comment on Modrinth does more for a small mod than anything else. It decides whether anyone ever finds it.

Open Modrinth

Tell your server

Ask whether client-side mods are allowed, then mention it in chat. That is how most players here found it.

Report bugs and ideas

Half the features in the mod came from players who simply said what was missing.
